The Sun

Basic Information

The Sun is the star at the center of our Solar System. It is a huge ball of extremely hot plasma made mostly of hydrogen and helium. The Sun provides the light and heat that make life on Earth possible. It contains more than 99% of the total mass of the Solar System.

Atmosphere & Temperature

The Sun has several layers, including the photosphere, chromosphere, and corona. The surface of the Sun is about 5,500°C (10,000°F), while the core reaches temperatures of around 15 million°C (27 million°F). The corona, the Sun's outer atmosphere, can also reach temperatures of millions of degrees.

Fun facts!

  • The Sun is a star, not a planet.
  • The Sun contains more than 99% of the mass in our Solar System.
  • Light from the Sun takes about 8 minutes to reach Earth.
  • The Sun is about 4.6 billion years old.
  • About 1.3 million Earths could fit inside the Sun.

Surface & Geography

What's your weight on the Sun?

Unlike planets such as Earth, the Sun does not have a solid surface. Instead, what we see as its surface is the photosphere, a layer of plasma that gives off most of the Sun's visible light. Below the photosphere are extremely hot layers that extend toward the Sun's core, where nuclear fusion produces the energy that powers the Sun.

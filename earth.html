Earth

Basic Information

Earth is the third planet from the Sun and the largest of the four terrestrial planets. It is the only planet currently known to support life and has a diverse environment containing oceans, continents, mountains, deserts, forests, and polar ice. Earth has a diameter of about 12,742 kilometers and is the only known planet with large amounts of stable liquid water on its surface.

Atmosphere & Temperature

Earth has an atmosphere made primarily of nitrogen and oxygen, with smaller amounts of other gases including carbon dioxide and water vapor. The atmosphere protects life from harmful radiation, helps regulate temperature, and allows liquid water to exist on the surface. Earth's climate varies greatly between different regions and includes deserts, tropical environments, temperate areas, and polar regions.

Surface & Geography

Earth's surface is constantly changing because of plate tectonics, erosion, volcanic activity, and other geological processes. The planet contains seven major continents and enormous oceans. Earth also has mountains, valleys, caves, volcanoes, glaciers, and deep ocean trenches. Mount Everest is the highest point above sea level, while the Mariana Trench contains some of the deepest known areas of Earth's oceans.
